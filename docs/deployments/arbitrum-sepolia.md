Nothing is deployed: every row below reads `not yet deployed`, and every value later written here is testnet-only and non-normative. This record holds observations of the Arbitrum Sepolia deployment made under [CIP-0004](../cip/0004-sepolia-testnet-configuration.md) after that CIP is Final. It sets no value, it is not a revision of CIP-0004, and nothing in it may be copied as a mainnet value or default. The seated maintainer fills it by an ordinary pull request.

The record is append-only. A redeployment adds new rows with their own date and never overwrites or deletes an earlier row. The model's manifest, `manifestHash`, license identifier and the sentence the registrant read are filled in CIP-0004's Model to register item before Last Call; that item is open in this draft, so they are not here. This record holds on-chain observations, operator settings, the contracts commit and the Semaphore artifact hashes checked before deployment.
